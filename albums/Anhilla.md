  ## Personal Notes


  - Sometimes feels like the first thing I made well. Quite proud of this, the
  planning that went into it, the use of themes/leitmotifs, the arrangements,
  the more creative aspects of the production. etc. etc.

  - A lot of this was mapped out in advance in demos, and then given to the rest
  of the band. We had rehearsal sessions (often unplugged in Matt's old
  Sheffield apt.). Some wonderful memories of working on this (and other flesh
  records) in that apartment. Furnace would be on, we'd have watched a horror
  movie, had a glass of wine or a cocktail. Just shooting the shit, talking
  about music we liked, what we could do that would be fun and different.
  Something fond to reflect on, especially given the general chaos involved with
  the rest of my life at that time.
