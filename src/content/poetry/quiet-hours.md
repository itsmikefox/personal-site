The house exhales its warmth at night,\
the clock forgets to keep its time,\
and somewhere past the kitchen light\
a page turns, patient, line by line.

I trade the noise of all the day\
for this: a lamp, a chair, a pen,\
the small and stubborn need to say\
a true thing once, and once again.
