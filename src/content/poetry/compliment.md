There they walk\
three women in compliment

Beth flowing in patterned floral\
Josie, hands tucked in her jumpsuit (beige)\
Alicia bowed over in knee high laughter

decolaged hair and crows feet\
a tangled mess of boots and sneakers and heels\
pack of cigs smashed into a shoulder bag for later.
