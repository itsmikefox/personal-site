---
title: "Midi"
pubDate: 2026-08-31
---

A feisty beast, this kitten\
she crawls through weft and tangled floss\
teeth nipping at my fifth finger\
but still, like a rubber bladder she bows into my arms

She cries every morning\
creating branching paths over our cork floors\
up into the window sills\
penned in like bound roots

Such bullshit\
that we don't let her run free\
to warm the neighborhood with havoc\
and sink blooded fangs into tawny feathers

I want nothing more than for her to rest on my elbow\
and rest her peach fuzz chin in the crook of my neck
