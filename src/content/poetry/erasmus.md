---
title: "Erasmus"
pubDate: 2026-07-12
---

after a long walk around the world, he picked up his pen.\
having had his heated debates with friends and colleagues over a couple bottles of wine\
looked out the window, chewed his nib\
Leaned his pockmarked head against a feather-frail hand\
then: thinking over it\
put the pen down.\
stood up from dad's old wooden desk\
and played darts.
