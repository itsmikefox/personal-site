---
title: "Late November"
pubDate: 2026-07-12
---

like a garden after a killing frost\
sick-green stems, and crumbling cell walls\
wrinkled squash and fallow zucchini\
in the permafrost

crystal palaces emerge from the dirt\
and are crushed under clean winter boots\
vapor clouds drawn into dry morning air.\
as longtoothed doubt settles in, like the mice in our walls.

meanwhile the furnaces kick on\
and gas is siponed through frosted iron\
those pinprick seeds are left to wait\
under a blanket of snow.
