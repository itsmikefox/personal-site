I had bought a polyphonic synthesizer. Plantasia was all the rage.

This was a fun exploration of theory and production techniques for me. I was watching a lot of [8-bit Music Theory](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCeZLO2VgbZHeDcongKzzfOw) and indulging myself on "in the box" music making.

## What I Learned

- Phasing
- Theme modifications
- Counterpoint
- Practical application of theory
- My first foray into drum programming

I was unemployed and would wake up, apply for jobs for three hours straight, and then launch into this in the afternoons.
