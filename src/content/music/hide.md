> Horror abounds. What dark doom calls to you from beyond the sepulcher's threshold? Descend the steps and meet your end.

Extremely fun to make, probably also the last time I used a wah pedal on a record.

## Finding the Sound

Album that shaped a lot of my perspective on "genre." This was a blast and a quick one to knock out. Matt had described Doom/Sludge metal to me as "slowcore but heavy" and I think I definitely took that a little too literally.

## Inspirations

Big inspirations for my writing/pacing on this came from:
- Yeah Wings
- God Jr.
- Pallbearer
- YOB
- Electric Wizard

## Reception

People in the world found this, and liked it and wrote about it. I continue to be grateful for that. I still get people writing to me about Flesh Of The Stars.
